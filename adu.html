ADUs & Guest Homes

ADU Construction in Los Angeles

More Space for Family, Guests or Income.

An accessory dwelling unit (ADU) adds a full, independent living space to your property: a home for family, a guest house, a home office or a rental. V&R Home Builders builds ADUs and garage conversions across Los Angeles, Orange County and the Inland Empire.

A typical ADU takes 6–12 months from start to finish, depending on the type of unit, its size and the permitting timeline. One team carries the project from planning through construction and final inspection.

Questions

Common Questions

How long does it take to build an ADU?
A typical ADU takes 6–12 months, depending on the type of unit, its size and the permitting timeline. We confirm a schedule with you before work begins.
What areas do you serve?
We work in all Los Angeles neighborhoods, including the Westside, Central LA and Downtown, the Eastside, the San Fernando Valley, Pasadena and the San Gabriel Valley, the South Bay and Long Beach, as well as Orange County and the Inland Empire.
Are you a licensed contractor?
Yes. V&R Home Builders Inc. is a California-licensed general contractor, CSLB License #1160807. You can verify the license on the Contractors State License Board website.
How is my project priced?
Every project is priced to its specific scope. After a site walkthrough, we provide a clear written proposal covering scope, timeline and investment, so there are no surprises.
