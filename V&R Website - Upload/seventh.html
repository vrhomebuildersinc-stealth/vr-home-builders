Whole Home Remodel

Project Overview

Craftsman Character, Modern Calm.

A complete reinvention of a classic craftsman bungalow. We wrapped the exterior in a deep, moody navy, then opened the interior into bright, gallery-white rooms grounded by warm herringbone flooring throughout.

The kitchen pairs rich walnut-toned cabinetry with a sage-green island and marble-look slab backsplash, while each bath carries its own statement tile—from emerald herringbone to warm wood-grain feature walls. Out back, a clean gravel yard and patio make easy space for evenings outdoors.
