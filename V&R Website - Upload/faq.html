Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Services & Areas
What areas do you serve?
We work in all Los Angeles neighborhoods, including the Westside, Central LA and Downtown, the Eastside, the San Fernando Valley, Pasadena and the San Gabriel Valley, the South Bay and Long Beach, as well as Orange County and the Inland Empire.
What types of projects do you take on?
Whole-home remodels, kitchen remodels, bathroom remodels, ADUs and guest homes, select custom homes and outdoor living spaces. See our whole-home, kitchen, bathroom and ADU pages for details.
Are you a licensed contractor?
Yes. V&R Home Builders Inc. is a California-licensed general contractor, CSLB License #1160807. You can verify the license on the Contractors State License Board website.
Timelines
How long does a kitchen remodel take?
A typical kitchen remodel takes 8–12 weeks.
How long does a bathroom remodel take?
A typical bathroom remodel takes 4–8 weeks.
How long does it take to build an ADU?
A typical ADU takes 6–12 months, depending on the type of unit, its size and the permitting timeline.
How long does a whole-home remodel take?
It depends on the size of the home and the scope of work. We include a detailed, project-specific schedule with every proposal.
What affects the timeline?
Mainly the scope of work, material and appliance lead times, and permitting. We confirm a realistic schedule with you before work begins and keep you updated throughout.
Working With Us
How do I get started?
Fill out the contact form or call us at (323) 492-6400. We start with a consultation, then visit the home to understand the space and your goals.
How is my project priced?
Every project is priced to its specific scope. After a site walkthrough, we provide a clear written proposal covering scope, timeline and investment, so there are no surprises.
Do you help with design and material selections?
Yes. We help plan layouts and guide selections, and we also work alongside your architect or designer if you already have one.
Do you handle permits?
Yes. We coordinate permits and inspections with your city as part of the project.
Can I live in my home during the remodel?
For single-room projects like a kitchen or bathroom, many clients stay in the home. For whole-home remodels, it depends on the scope; we will talk through what to expect during your consultation.
