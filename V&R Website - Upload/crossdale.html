Kitchen & Bath Remodel

Project Overview

Warm Oak, Soft Light, Quiet Luxury.

A focused kitchen and bathroom renovation built around natural materials. We reworked the kitchen layout with rift-cut white oak cabinetry, a breakfast bar, and handmade-look vertical tile that runs from counter to ceiling, finished with brushed-brass hardware and a pot filler over the range.

In the bath, the same calm palette carries through: a sculptural round soaking tub, wall-mounted brass fixtures, oak vanity, and a tiled arched niche—simple, tactile, and made to age beautifully.
