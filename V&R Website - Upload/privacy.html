Privacy Notice

Effective September 24, 2026

V&R Home Builders Inc. (“V&R,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This notice explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you submit our contact form, email, or call us, we collect what you choose to share—such as your name, email address, phone number, project type, project location, estimated budget, and message.
  • Basic technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record standard log data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited. If we use website analytics, it is used only in aggregate to understand how the site is used.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry, schedule consultations, and prepare proposals.
  • To communicate with you about your project.
  • To operate, secure, and improve this website.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

How we share it

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only with service providers that help us run our business—such as our website host, form-processing service, and email provider—who may use it only to provide those services to us, or when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information only as long as needed to respond to you and manage any resulting project, or as required for our business and legal records.

Your choices and California rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you have provided, or to stop contacting you, at any time. California residents may have additional rights under California law, including the right to know what personal information we collect and to request its deletion. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information (such as financial account numbers) through the contact form.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last revised.
