Kitchen Remodeling

Kitchen Remodeling in Los Angeles

Kitchens Built for Living and Gathering.

The kitchen is where a remodel is felt most. V&R Home Builders designs and builds kitchens across Los Angeles that balance how you cook, gather and store with finishes that hold up to daily life.

A typical kitchen remodel takes 8–12 weeks. From layout changes and custom cabinetry to counters, backsplash, lighting and appliances, one team plans and builds it, and you get a clear schedule before work begins.

Questions

Common Questions

How long does a kitchen remodel take?
A typical kitchen remodel takes 8–12 weeks. The exact schedule depends on the scope, material lead times and permitting, and we confirm it with you before work begins.
What areas do you serve?
We work in all Los Angeles neighborhoods, including the Westside, Central LA and Downtown, the Eastside, the San Fernando Valley, Pasadena and the San Gabriel Valley, the South Bay and Long Beach, as well as Orange County and the Inland Empire.
Are you a licensed contractor?
Yes. V&R Home Builders Inc. is a California-licensed general contractor, CSLB License #1160807. You can verify the license on the Contractors State License Board website.
How is my project priced?
Every project is priced to its specific scope. After a site walkthrough, we provide a clear written proposal covering scope, timeline and investment, so there are no surprises.
