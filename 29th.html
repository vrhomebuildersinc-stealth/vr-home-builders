Project Overview

A Craftsman, Beautifully Restored.

A full restoration and remodel of a historic Los Angeles Craftsman, honoring its original character while rebuilding it for modern life. A moody charcoal exterior sets the tone, while inside we restored the wainscoting and fireplace and introduced a striking green shaker kitchen with natural stone and warm brass.

Every room was reimagined—from the light-filled living and dining spaces to the serene bedrooms and nursery—finished by a landscaped backyard with raised garden beds and string-lit gathering areas.
